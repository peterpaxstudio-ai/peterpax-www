Law firms that run regular consultations lose hours every month to booking by phone, and double bookings and no-shows still happen.

Bookings 24/7, without the front desk

Three parts that work together: from the client's click to an event in the firm's calendar.

A Polish-language booking form

The client sees available dates and times, picks a slot and fills in the form in Polish.

Automatic availability check

The system checks your Google Calendar in real time. Only free slots are visible, so a double booking is impossible.

Confirmation with a Google Meet link

The client gets an email with the date, topic and link within seconds. Your team gets a notification with the client's details.

Three steps, no downtime

  1. The client picks a slot

    They open the page, see a calendar with free dates, choose a time and fill in the form. It works around the clock.

  2. The system books it

    It checks the calendar, creates the event with the client's details and generates a Google Meet link. It takes a few seconds.

  3. Confirmations go out on their own

    The client gets an email with the link, the team gets a notification, and the event is ready in the calendar. No manual work.

Scope and add-ons

You start with what you need. We match the scope to the number of specialists and consultation topics.

Starter

A Polish-language form, availability check in Google Calendar, an email confirmation, a Google Meet link and a team notification.

Starter + Routing

The client picks a consultation topic (for example income tax, VAT, payroll) and is assigned automatically to the right specialist, who gets a separate notification. Most often chosen by firms with two or more specialists.

Full

Everything from Starter and Routing plus one extension module of your choice: SMS reminders, cancelling and rescheduling, or CRM integration.

SMS reminders

An SMS to the client the day before the consultation. The client can confirm or cancel by replying.

Cancelling and rescheduling

A link in the email lets the client change the time without phoning the front desk. The system frees the slot itself.

A booking log in a Google Sheet

Every booking goes into a sheet: date, client, topic, specialist, status.

Setup in one working day

No downtime and no IT work on your side.

Access to your Google Calendar

We need OAuth2 authorization to the firm's calendar, mail settings to send confirmations and 30 minutes to go through time slots and consultation topics. We take care of the rest.

The form on your domain

We embed the form on the firm's website or provide it on a subpage. The client never leaves your environment.

GDPR and client data

Booking data is stored only in the firm's Google Calendar, with no external databases. A GDPR clause can be added to the form.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my calendar?

No. SmartCal works on your Google Calendar, and the client only sees free slots.

Will the form be in Polish?

Yes, the client gets the form and messages in Polish.

Where is my clients' data?

Booking data goes only to your company's Google Calendar, with no external databases.

How long does setup take?

One working day, with no downtime.
