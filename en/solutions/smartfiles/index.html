Where did the quote for client X from last Tuesday go?

Your salespeople send dozens of quotes a week. When you ask for a particular file or a month's summary, a nervous dig through inboxes and “Downloads” folders begins.

Scattered across laptops

Every salesperson keeps files on their own desktop. When someone is on leave, nobody else can see the quote that was sent.

A mess of names

quote_corrected_v2.pdf, final-quote(1).pdf. No standard, no dates, no client name in the file title.

No central register

A quotes sheet either does not exist or nobody fills it in. Management cannot see how many quotes go out.

Follow-ups that slip away

Quotes are left unanswered, and salespeople forget to call after 5 days.

A solution that needs no change of habits

You do not force your team to key data into clunky systems. SmartFiles works in the background of the email program (Thunderbird, Outlook, webmail) and needs nothing installed on employees' computers.

A standard file name

The pattern YYYY-MM-DD_Sender_Subject.pdf, for example 2026-11-18_JanKowalski_FirmaABC.pdf.

Everything in one folder

A copy of every quote goes to a secure folder on your company's Google Drive.

A register in a Google Sheet

A new row appears at once: date, who sent it, to whom and a direct link to preview the PDF.

How SmartFiles works in 4 steps

No extra software to install on employees' computers.

  1. The salesperson sends an email

    They send the quote with a PDF attachment exactly as before, from their own email program.

  2. Automatic detection

    In a fraction of a second the system catches the new message and checks keywords (quote, offer) and whether a PDF is attached.

  3. Standardizing and Google Drive

    SmartFiles standardizes the file name and saves a copy to a folder in the cloud.

  4. An entry in the sheet

    A new row with a link to preview the PDF appears in the central sheet at once.
