Two ways of working

Manager Dashboard (offline)

For staff only. Clients have no access, the page is not indexed, and competitors cannot peek at your rates or margins. Orders are saved in the browser's memory, so it also works without internet and lets you print dish sheets for the kitchen.

Client card (online)

The client composes the catering on a smartphone, via a link or QR code. You get a ready order.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my website or till system?

No. SmartMenu works independently, as a link, a local dashboard or a QR code.

Does the dashboard work without internet?

Yes. The Manager Dashboard saves orders in the browser's memory and lets you print dish sheets for the kitchen with no network at all.

Will clients see my rates and margins?

Not in the internal mode. Clients have no access to the system, and the page is not indexed by search engines.
