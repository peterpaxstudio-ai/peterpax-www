Four steps, no magic, a well-organized process

Every email follows the same path, in the same order. The AI prepares, a human approves.

  1. The email arrives

    The system receives the message over IMAP from your current mailbox, without moving your email.

  2. The AI reads and understands

    It sets category and urgency and writes the summary, in 3-8 seconds.

  3. Priority and folder

    Critical matters are flagged at once, the rest goes to the right folders.

  4. A draft waits for a click

    A ready reply waits before you open your email.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI send emails for me?

No. No email is deleted or sent without a human. A draft always waits for your approval.

Do I have to change my mailbox or email program?

No. The system works over IMAP from your current mailbox, without migrating email.

How long does setup take?

Typically about 2 days from the call.

How many hours a week does your team lose to email?

Send us 20-30 sample emails (you can anonymize them). We will show how the system sorts them and what draft replies it prepares. You get the result in 48 hours, with no obligation.
