Your part is 30 seconds in the morning, the system does the rest

  1. In the morning you enter the dish

    You type the dish of the day and number of portions in a simple form on your phone.

  2. The system publishes the post

    It posts on Facebook by itself and updates the Messenger bot.

  3. The customer orders

    They pick a quantity and give an address and time. The order lands on the shared list and in the kitchen as a notification, and the customer gets a confirmation at once.

  4. The bot closes sales

    When portions run out, the bot replies “Sold out for today” and stops taking orders.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change how the kitchen works?

No. You get a notification and a list of orders. In the morning you spend 30 seconds entering the dish of the day.

Do customers have to install an app?

No. They order in Messenger, which they already use.

What about customers who prefer to call?

Whoever is at the bar enters a phone order in the same form, so everything lands on one list.

Do I pay a commission on orders?

No. It is your own channel, with no percentage of the order.

Is there a long contract?

No. We propose a 30-day pilot on real orders. If you do not see fewer calls and less retyping, we end it with no obligation.
