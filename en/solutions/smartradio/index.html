You know that Monday

This is not radio work. It is retyping.

Dedications from three directions

They arrive by form, on WhatsApp and in comments. Someone collects them, reads them, throws out spam, checks whether the track is in the library and passes them to the presenter.

The same release three times

Someone retypes a press release into a news item, a Facebook post and a text to read on air.

The advertiser report

Put together in Excel at the end of the month. Or not at all.

What exactly we do

An inbox for dedications and song requests

The form, WhatsApp and Facebook go into one queue. Messages are sorted (dedication, song request, fault, contact with the newsroom), spam and profanity are filtered out, and the listener gets a confirmation. The presenter gets a ready list for the show.

From a press release to publication

The label sends a release, and you get a draft news item for the website, a Facebook and Instagram post and a 30-second text for the air. The newsroom edits and approves. Nothing goes out without a human.

An advertiser report

Every month a PDF for the client: which spots, at what times, how many times. You sell, the system counts. The client sees what they pay for and renews more easily.

Extras, if they help

A Top 10 chart as ready content for the whole week, a “what is playing now” bot on WhatsApp, a daily music quiz, and short captioned clips cut from show recordings.

Frequently asked questions

Does anything go on air or online without approval?

No. Draft news items, posts and on-air texts are edited and approved by the newsroom. Nothing goes out without a human.

What about listeners' data?

We process listeners' data under a data processing agreement (GDPR).

What if it does not pay off for you?

At the audit we calculate together: who, how much, how often. If the calculation shows that the solution will not pay off, we will say so plainly.
