Plans matched to the size of your business

Basic

For a single location starting to build its Google presence. One profile, real-time notifications, 20 ready reply templates, a reviews widget for your website.

Standard

For businesses that want to actively collect reviews, not only react. Up to 3 profiles, an AI assistant with reply suggestions, SMS and email campaigns, sentiment and trend analysis.

Pro

For chains and agencies. Up to 15 profiles, full AI autopilot mode, CRM and POS integrations, multiple user roles, a dedicated account manager and a white-label option.

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI replies sound artificial?

The system learns your company's tone and suggests replies you can always edit before sending. You decide what goes public and when.

Will it work with my CRM or POS system?

In the Pro plan, yes, with integrations for Salesforce, HubSpot and popular restaurant systems. In Basic and Standard you export data and connect it through Zapier.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, on the monthly plan with no notice period.
