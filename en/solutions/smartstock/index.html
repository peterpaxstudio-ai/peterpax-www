How the system works in 4 simple steps

No learning new programs. You work with tools you know.

  1. Taking the order

    The client calls or fills in a form. You enter the client, goods, quantity and delivery address.

  2. A notification on the phone

    An instruction with ready buttons lands on the warehouse worker's Telegram.

  3. Confirming the release

    The worker loads the goods and taps “Released”. The order status changes to completed at once.

  4. Stock and the boss's report

    Stock comes off the database, a release log is saved, and if anything runs low the owner gets an alert right away.

5 modules that make up your system

Everything configured, connected and tested for the specifics of your business.

Order intake

A simple form for the office or the client, an automatic order number and a register in the cloud.

Telegram notifications

A dedicated bot sends release instructions to workers' phones. Buttons let them change the status without opening sheets.

Stock levels and alerts

An item database linked to releases. When stock falls below an agreed minimum, the owner gets an alert.

A daily owner's report

A daily summary at 18:00 (or another time): releases, number of completed orders and critical stock levels.

Release log and audit

A chronological record of every physical release of goods. No more ambiguity.

Care and hosting

Frequently asked questions

Will my employees cope with the system?

Yes. A warehouse worker does not have to learn any complicated programs or log in to panels. They get a Telegram message and tap one button: “Released”. If they can send a text message, they can use it from day one.

Do I have to buy expensive servers or licences?

No. It is all based on Google Sheets and a server we manage. You do not pay for every user or any hidden licence fees.

How long does setup take and will it stop the company's work?

Up to 7 working days. We configure the system in parallel, without disturbing your current work.

What if we want to extend the system later?

At any time we can add invoicing, an online shop integration, a barcode or QR module, or an SMS gateway.
