Google added features to Gmail and Calendar in which Gemini proposes free times itself. They speed up arranging meetings, but they do not replace a form where a client picks a slot on their own at any hour. Below is what is known about these features and what follows for a small business.

## What Google added

- **Help me schedule in Gmail (October 2025).** According to a [TechCrunch report from 14 October 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/14/googles-gemini-can-now-help-you-schedule-google-calendar-meetings/), a button below the compose window shows your free time slots. You can edit them, insert them into the email and send it. When the recipient picks a time, the invite lands on both calendars. At launch the feature was meant for one-on-one meetings.
- **Suggested times in Calendar (January 2026).** According to a [Telepolis report from 27 January 2026](https://www.telepolis.pl/tech/aplikacje/kalendarz-google-nowa-funkcja-spotkania-terminy-dla-wszystkich) (in Polish), Gemini in Calendar compares attendees' calendars, working hours and conflicts, and when you create an event it shows the best times for the whole group. If many invitees decline, the calendar can propose another time. According to that report the feature is on by default for Google Workspace accounts, including Business Standard and Plus and Enterprise Standard and Plus.

## What this gives a small business

- Less email back and forth when arranging a meeting with a contractor.
- A faster way to find a time that suits the whole team.
- Automatic invites on both calendars.

## What it does not solve

- **Self-service booking by a client.** These are features for arranging meetings between people who have calendars. The client still has to reply to an email. There is no form where they pick a free slot on their own at 9 pm.
- **Every account.** Some features are tied to paid Workspace plans. Check availability and language in your own account, because I did not confirm them for a Polish interface.
- **Routing to the right person.** Gemini does not know that a tax matter should go to a different specialist than a payroll one.
- **Reminders and cancellations.** A calendar does not cut missed appointments the way an SMS reminder and a simple reschedule link do.

## What to do about it

If you arrange a few meetings a week, Google's features are enough. If you run regular consultations, clients call after hours and double bookings happen, you need a booking form connected to your calendar.
