SmartInbox is an inbox assistant for offices and small businesses. Every incoming email is read, given a priority and filed in the right folder, and simple questions get a draft reply waiting. You just read and click "Send".

## Sound familiar?

- 120 emails are waiting, and one of them is from a tax office with a 7-day deadline.
- Sorting email takes hours. According to the solution's description, offices can lose 40-80 hours a month to it.
- A client is waiting for an answer because their question sank among newsletters.
- You answer the same question again, in the same words.

## What SmartInbox does

- **Reads** every incoming email and writes a two-sentence summary.
- **Sets a category and priority,** for example income tax, VAT, social insurance, inspection.
- **Files it in folders** before anyone opens the inbox.
- **Catches urgent matters** from official domains and deadlines hidden in the text, and the boss gets an instant alert.
- **Prepares a draft reply** to standard client questions, based on your company's knowledge base.

No email is deleted or sent without a human. The AI prepares, you approve.

## How it works in 4 steps

1. **The email arrives.** The system receives the message over IMAP from your current mailbox, without moving your email.
2. **The AI reads and understands.** It sets the category and urgency and writes the summary. According to the description, this takes 3-8 seconds from the moment the email arrives.
3. **Priority and folder.** Critical matters are flagged at once, the rest goes to the right folders.
4. **A draft waits for a click.** Your colleague reads the prepared reply, edits it if needed and sends it.

## What setup looks like

- **Day 0:** a 30-minute call. We go through the kinds of matters in your office and get IMAP access to the mailbox.
- **Days 1-2:** configuration on our side. We set categories, priorities, alerts and the knowledge base to fit your processes.
- **From day 2:** the inbox works on its own. Your team opens a sorted inbox in the morning.

No system replacement, no email migration, no week-long training.

## What you gain

- **Time:** no more manual sorting, a summary on every email and ready draft replies.
- **Peace of mind:** letters from offices and other critical deadlines do not get lost in the crowd.
- **Reputation:** new enquiries and client questions get an answer in minutes, not days.

## Who it is for

- Offices and firms that get a lot of email, including from authorities and clients.
- Teams where the same kind of question comes back every day.
- Owners who do not want to change their mailbox or learn a new program.

## Who it is not for

- If you get a few emails a day, sorting by hand is enough.
- If you want the AI to send replies without your control. SmartInbox does not do that, a human always approves.
