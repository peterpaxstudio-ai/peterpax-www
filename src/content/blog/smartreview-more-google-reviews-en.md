SmartReview is a Google review management system for small businesses. After a visit or purchase it asks clients for a review, and for every new review it drafts a reply in your company's tone. Nothing goes public without your approval.

## Know this problem?

- According to the solution's description, 90% of customers check Google reviews before they call or visit. Your profile is your home page today.
- A happy client pays, says thanks and goes home. Nobody remembers to leave a review.
- An unhappy client always remembers and writes a long one-star review that every following client sees.
- You have no time to ask for reviews or to answer every comment.

## What SmartReview does

- **Asks for a review** automatically after a visit or purchase, by email or SMS, without your involvement.
- **Drafts a reply** to every review in your company's tone. You can always edit it before it is sent.
- **Notifies you in real time** about new reviews, so you react to a complaint quickly, not a week later.
- **Shows reviews on your website** with a widget (depending on the plan).

## How it works in 3 steps

1. **You connect your Google profile.** It takes about 5 minutes, with no technical knowledge. One, three or fifteen locations, depending on the plan.
2. **The system asks clients for a review.** Automatically after a visit or purchase.
3. **The AI prepares replies.** Replies in your style wait for your approval.

## What you gain

- **Time:** you do not write replies from scratch, and review requests go out on their own.
- **Reputation:** every review has a reply, and the profile looks actively run.
- **Control:** you decide what goes public and when.

An example from one rollout, Standard plan, according to data on the solution's page: the average Google rating rose from 3.8 to 4.6 in 3 months, and the number of reviews doubled. This is one client's result, so it is not a promise for every business.

## Who it is for

- Restaurants, salons, workshops, clinics and other businesses whose clients look for them on Google.
- Owners who have no time for regular review requests and replies.
- Businesses with one or several locations.

## Who it is not for

- If you get a few reviews a year, replying by hand is enough.
- If you want the AI to reply publicly without your approval. SmartReview does not work that way, you decide what goes public.

## Plans

- **Basic:** for a single location that is starting to build its Google presence. One profile, real-time notifications, ready reply templates, a reviews widget for your website.
- **Standard:** for businesses that want to actively collect reviews. Up to 3 profiles, an AI assistant with reply suggestions, SMS and email campaigns, trend analysis.
- **Pro:** for chains and agencies. Up to 15 profiles, full AI autopilot, CRM and POS integrations, multiple user roles.
