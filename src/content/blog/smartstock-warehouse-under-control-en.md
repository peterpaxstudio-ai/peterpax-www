SmartStock is a simple system for orders and stock levels for small and medium businesses, without expensive ERP systems. An order comes into a form, the warehouse worker gets a Telegram notification and confirms the release with one click, and stock updates itself.

## Sound familiar?

- Phone orders end up on paper slips that get lost or cannot be read.
- The owner or forwarder runs to the warehouse to pass on instructions.
- A client asks about availability, and you have to physically check the yard or the hall.
- Nobody knows who released the goods or whether the order was shipped at all.

The result is financial losses, delivery delays and constant stress for the owner.

## What SmartStock does

- **A clean order form:** 15 seconds to enter an order, which goes straight into the register with a status and a unique number.
- **The instruction in your pocket:** the warehouse worker gets a Telegram message with the client's details and the goods.
- **Automatic stock correction and shortage alerts:** a release reduces stock by itself, and when goods run low the boss gets an alert.
- **The end of the day in one report:** at 18:00 a summary of what was released, which orders were completed and whether everything is fine.

## How it works in 4 steps

1. **Taking the order.** The client calls or fills in a form, and you enter the client, goods, quantity and delivery address.
2. **A notification on the phone.** The warehouse worker gets an instruction with ready buttons.
3. **Confirming the release.** The worker loads the goods and taps “Released”. The order status changes to completed at once.
4. **Stock and the boss's report.** Stock comes off the database, a release log is saved, and if anything runs low the owner gets an alert right away.

## What it is made of

The system has five modules that we configure and connect together:

- order intake,
- Telegram notifications,
- stock levels and low-stock alerts,
- a daily report for the owner,
- a release log and audit of operations (who released what, how much and when).

On top of that comes technical care: a dedicated, secure server, constant monitoring, backups and updates.

## What you gain

- **Control:** you know exactly who released the goods, in what quantity and at what minute.
- **Peace of mind:** you do not run around the warehouse or check stock on the yard.
- **Order:** no paperwork, and every order has a number and a status.

## Who it is for

- Businesses that release goods from a warehouse, yard or hall and take orders by phone or form.
- Owners who want to know what left and when, without rolling out a heavy system.
- Teams where the warehouse worker has a phone but does not want to learn a new program.

## Who it is not for

- If you need a full WMS (shelf locations, scanners, hundreds of orders a day), that is a different level. SmartStock is a simple flow of orders and stock.
- If you have a few orders a month, a notebook or a sheet is enough.
