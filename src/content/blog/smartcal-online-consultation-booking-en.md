SmartCal is automatic consultation booking for law firms and other businesses that see clients by appointment. The client picks a free slot in a Polish-language form, and you get a ready event in your Google Calendar with an online meeting link. No phone calls and no work for the front desk.

## Sound familiar?

- According to the solution's description, law firms can lose about 20 hours a month to phone calls about appointments. Each booking is 3-5 minutes of talking, entering it in the calendar and sending a confirmation.
- With manual handling, double bookings happen. Two people at the same time is an awkward situation, and the client leaves with a worse impression.
- Clients want to book after 5 pm, when the office is closed. Some give up instead of waiting until morning.

## What SmartCal does

- **A Polish-language booking form.** The client sees available dates and times, picks a slot and fills in the form in Polish.
- **Automatic availability check.** The system checks your Google Calendar in real time. Only free slots are visible, so a double booking is impossible.
- **Confirmation with a Google Meet link.** The client gets an email with the date, topic and meeting link within seconds. Your team gets a notification with the client's details.

## How it works in 3 steps

1. **The client picks a slot.** They open the page, see a calendar with free dates, choose a time and fill in the form. It works around the clock.
2. **The system books the slot.** It checks Google Calendar, creates the event with the client's details and generates a Google Meet link. It takes a few seconds.
3. **Confirmations go out automatically.** The client gets an email with the link, the team gets a notification, and the event is ready in the calendar.

## What you gain

- **Time:** nobody answers calls about appointments or enters bookings by hand.
- **Peace of mind:** no more double bookings.
- **Clients:** those who want to book in the evening or at the weekend do it at once, instead of giving up.

## Plans and add-ons

- **Starter:** a Polish-language booking form, availability check in Google Calendar, an email confirmation for the client, an automatic Google Meet link and a notification for the team. Pricing depends on the number of specialists and slots.
- **Starter + Routing:** the same, plus the client chooses a consultation topic (for example income tax, VAT, payroll) and is automatically assigned to the right specialist, who gets a separate notification. Most often chosen by firms with two or more specialists.
- **Full:** everything from Starter and Routing plus one extension module of your choice: SMS reminders, cancellation or rescheduling, or CRM integration.
- **Add-ons for any plan:** SMS reminders a day before the consultation (the client can confirm or cancel by replying), a link in the confirmation email to cancel or reschedule without phoning, and a booking log in a Google Sheet.

You will find current prices and plan details on the [SmartCal page](https://peterpax.pl/smartcal/) (in Polish).

## Who it is for

- Law firms, clinics and advisory firms that see clients for scheduled consultations.
- Teams with two or more specialists who need to route clients to the right person.
- Owners who use Google Calendar.

## Who it is not for

- If you arrange a few meetings a week and have them under control, a normal calendar is enough.
- If you do not use Google Calendar. SmartCal is built on it.
